HOW TO PLAY - SPIDER-MAN SNAKES & LADDERS

🚫

No Snakes & No Ladders!

Despite the title, there are zero snakes and zero ladders on this board! Instead, Spider-Men web-pull you, Green Goblins kidnap you on hoverboards, and black-hole Portals warp you higher!

👥

2 Players — Single Device

Two human players (MJ-1 and MJ-2) take turns on the same laptop or mobile phone. Same appearance, distinguished by their Auburn Red and Electric Cyan hair!

🎯

Exact 100 Win Rule

To reach 100, you must roll EXACTLY the required number! If you are on Tile 99, you need an exact roll of 1. If your roll exceeds 100, you cannot move and your turn ends.

🎲

Bonus Rolls

Roll a 6 to earn an immediate bonus roll! Also, landing on the same tile as the other player grants an immediate bonus roll (both players remain on the tile, nobody is displaced).

🕷️

6 Spider-Men (Gold Spider Legs)

6 Spider-Men with blue-and-red suits and gold spider legs on their backs. Landing on a trigger initiates a cinematic web pull pulling MJ across the board to Spider-Man's tile!

🎃

3 Green Goblins (Hoverboards)

3 Green Goblins patrol the board standing on flying hoverboards. Landing on a Goblin triggers an airborne kidnapping that drops MJ to a lower position!

🌀

6 Portals (3 Connected Pairs)

Three entrance portals and three exit portals. Entering an entrance portal pulls MJ into a dark-black-hole gravitational singularity, emerging from the corresponding exit portal at a higher position (never downward)!

🐙

Tile 100 — Doctor Octopus Trap!

Reaching 100 is ALWAYS a trap! When MJ reaches Tile 100, Doctor Octopus appears with mechanical tentacles, grabs MJ, and repeatedly leaps away across the skyline into the unknown. The player who reached 100 loses: "YOU LOSE - You fell right into their trap."
