HOW TO PLAY

🎲

Standard Rolling

Roll the 3D dice to advance across the 100 numbered tiles. Rolling a 6 grants an immediate BONUS ROLL!

💥

Player Collisions

Multiple MJs can occupy the same tile! If you land on another player's square, you receive a BONUS ROLL!

🕸️

5 Spider-Men Web Pulls

There are 5 Classic Blue & Red Spider-Men standing on the board. Each has a secret trigger tile randomized each game. Land on it, and Spidey shoots a visible 3D web line to reel you up to him!

🎃

6 Green Goblin Hoverboard Kidnappings

There are 6 Green Goblins hovering on high-tech gliders. Land directly on a Goblin, and he scoops you onto his hoverboard, flies you across the sky, and drops you at a random lower tile!

❓

The Secret 100 Rule

At the start of every match, the multiverse decides in secret: Does reaching 100 mean VICTORY or TOTAL DEFEAT? You will only find out when someone reaches it!
