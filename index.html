HOW TO PLAY - SPIDER-MAN SNAKES & LADDERS

🚫

No Snakes & No Ladders!

Despite the title, there are zero snakes and zero ladders on this board! Instead, Spider-Men web-pull you, Green Goblins kidnap you on hoverboards, and black-hole Portals warp you higher!

👥

2 Players — Single Device

Two human players (MJ-1 and MJ-2) take turns on the same laptop or mobile phone. Simply pass and play!

🎯

Exact 100 Win Rule

To win, you must land EXACTLY on Tile 100! If you are on Tile 99, you need an exact roll of 1. If your roll exceeds 100, you cannot move and forfeit your turn!

🕷️

6 Spider-Men (Gold Spider Legs)

6 Spider-Men with blue-and-red suits and gold spider legs on their backs. Landing on a trigger initiates a cinematic web pull pulling MJ across the board to Spider-Man's tile!

🎃

3 Green Goblins (Hoverboards)

3 Green Goblins patrol the board standing on flying hoverboards. Landing on a Goblin triggers an airborne kidnapping that drops MJ to a lower position!

🌀

4 Portals (2 Connected Pairs)

Two entrance portals and two exit portals. Entering an entrance portal pulls MJ into a dark-black-hole gravitational anomaly, emerging from the corresponding exit portal at a higher position (never downward)!

⚡

Tile 100 Climax: Mega Green Goblin & Black Spider-Man

Reaching 100 opens an aerial rift. A gigantic Mega Green Goblin emerges on a war-glider: "Right into the trap." She grabs MJ and hurls her off the building! Suddenly, Black Spider-Man swings in from the skyline, defeats the Mega Goblin, dives mid-air to rescue MJ, and crowns her winner!
